# SkillSphere – User Journey

## One Clear Path Through the Website

The student journey moves from discovery to connection.

SkillSphere provides a simple path for students to explore learning opportunities, discover instructors, create an account and contact the platform.

### 1. Home
**Start with a clear welcome and navigation.**

Students visit the homepage to understand SkillSphere and navigate through the available sections of the website.

### 2. Explore Courses
**Browse learning options and resources.**

Students explore different courses and learning areas to discover topics that match their interests.

### 3. View Instructors
**Understand who supports each course.**

Students can view instructor information and learn about the people associated with different courses.

### 4. Login / Signup
**Move toward a personalized account experience.**

Existing students can log in, while new students can sign up for an account.

### 5. Contact
**Reach out with questions or requests.**

Students can contact SkillSphere to share their questions, queries or requests.

---

## Student Journey Flow

**Home → Explore Courses → View Instructors → Login / Signup → Contact**

## Conclusion

The SkillSphere user journey is designed to provide students with a clear and simple website experience, from discovering courses to connecting with the platform.
